Research

Research Fellow at LSE STICERD, working with Professor Xavier Jaravel on the adoption of generative AI and what it changes for people, firms and policy.

Current research

Careers: how AI changes the advice we give young people
How generative AI reshapes which jobs and studies young people should choose, and how careers guidance should respond.
Access: bringing frontier AI to the many
Who is left out of the best AI tools, why, and what closes the gap. Democratising the frontier rather than concentrating it.
Productivity: how AI reaches firms
Measuring how AI affects firm productivity and growth through the tools and workflows that integrator firms build on top of it.
AI-led interview platforms
Principal designer of AI-led voice-interview studies measuring AI adoption among managers and workers, in partnership with the Resolution Foundation and the British Chambers of Commerce.
