Research

I work on the adoption of generative AI and what it changes for people, firms and policy. Three questions in particular:

  • Careers. How AI changes the advice we give young people about which jobs and studies to choose.
  • Access. How to bring frontier AI to the many, not the few: who is left out of the best tools, and what closes the gap.
  • Productivity. How AI reaches firm productivity and growth through the tools and workflows that integrator firms build on top of it.
