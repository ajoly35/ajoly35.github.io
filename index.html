Background

I am currently a Research Fellow at LSE STICERD, working with Professor Xavier Jaravel — much of it on generative AI: designing AI-led interview studies measuring how managers and workers are adopting generative AI, with the Resolution Foundation and the British Chambers of Commerce, and building a firm-level measure of GenAI adoption in product innovation from Revelio and Nielsen data with Xavier and Josh Feng.

BSc Econometrics & Mathematical Economics, First Class Honours, LSE. Exchange year at Harvard University (2024–2025).
