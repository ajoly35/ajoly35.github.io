Research

My research explores how AI is implemented inside companies and how it transforms them, and what that implies upstream for young people, education and policy. Five themes:

  • Entry-level hiring. The changing role of juniors when AI produces the first draft.
  • What students should learn. The skills that matter in the AI transition, and what schools and universities should teach.
  • Guiding students into careers. Careers guidance informed by how AI is transforming occupations.
  • Access. Bringing frontier AI to the many in a way that is compute-efficient and cost-effective.
  • Universities and government. How AI can close the timing gap between academic research and government analysis.
